RAG Fix - Step 4: Reranking
==============================

What we did
------------
The Cohere reranker was configured in the code but never actually
called - the settings existed but nothing used them. Wired it up:

- Retrieval now fetches a wider set of candidates (45 instead of 20)
- Those get passed through the confidence filter, same as before
- Then a second pass (Cohere reranker) re-scores everything specifically
  against the question asked, not just general similarity
- Only the top 6 highest-scoring chunks after that go to the AI

Also fixed a bug found while testing: three different places in the code
had a hardcoded chunk count that was silently overriding the reranker's
settings, so the new tighter top-6 selection wasn't actually reaching
the AI at first. Fixed all three.

Test we ran
------------
Document: Protocol_Ulcerative Colitis.pdf (same one from Steps 1-2)
Ran 5 questions - reused 3 from Step 2 (as a regression check) and
added 2 new ones designed to specifically test whether reranking helps
pull together answers that are scattered across multiple sections.

What we found
--------------
Reranking ran successfully on every single question, no failures.

The reranker's scoring is much sharper than plain similarity search was.
Example: for "DSMC or SRC?", the two actually-relevant chunks scored
0.99 and 0.75, while everything else dropped below 0.001. Before
reranking, all the candidate chunks scored close together (0.39-0.59)
and couldn't be told apart this clearly.

The two new "scattered answer" questions worked well:
- Pregnancy question: pulled together all 5 relevant pieces (what to
  do immediately, reporting rules, follow-up duration, what counts as
  a serious event) correctly and completely.
- Exclusion criteria question: correctly assembled 8 separate numbered
  criteria spread across 3 different pages, plus related context from
  a different section - exactly the kind of "answer scattered across
  the document" case reranking is meant to fix.

The 3 reused questions from Step 2 still answered correctly - no
regression from adding reranking.

Something to note for later
------------------------------
A couple of source quotes came back with odd leading text like "1 = "
or "3.3, 3 = " - looks like an artifact from how a table in the
document (the change-history table) gets parsed during ingestion, not
something the AI made up. Worth a closer look during a future
ingestion/parsing pass, not blocking this step.
